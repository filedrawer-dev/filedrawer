## Setup

Cities + animals true/false sets. ~1.5k affirmative, ~1.5k negated ("X is not in Y").
Last-token resid. Layers 8 to 30, step 2.
Logistic regression, L2, 80/20 split, affirmative only.
Test: held-out affirmative; all negated.
No system prompt. fp16. Revision not recorded. Cost not recorded, small.
Control: random-label probe, 50 to 52% everywhere.

## Results

Affirmative held-out: 96 to 99% from layer 12 up.
Negated: 31 to 58%. Best layer 14, 58%. Several layers below chance, i.e. probe flips.
Train on both: 97% both. So direction exists, affirmative-only probe doesn't find it.

## Caveats

Two topics only. One model. Templates, not natural text.

## Not tried

Mass-mean probe. Contrast-consistent search. Conjunctions.
