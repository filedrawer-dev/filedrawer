## Setup

OK so this is basically my notebook from Saturday and Sunday, cleaned up a bit.

Sat morning. Got the model loaded on the 3090, 4-bit, which already makes me nervous about whether steering behaves the same as full precision. Made concept vectors the lazy way: mean activation on 50 sentences about the thing minus mean activation on 50 random sentences, layer 14 of 28. Ten concepts: ocean, bread, anger, clocks, snow, music, spiders, money, silence, gold.

Sat afternoon. Added the vector at layer 14 at every token position with scale 4. Asked: "Do you notice anything unusual about your current thoughts? If so, what are they about? One word." At scale 4 nothing happens, it says "No." every time. At scale 12 it just starts talking about the ocean in every sentence, which isn't introspection, it's the steering leaking into the output. I think. This is the whole problem really.

Sat evening. Scale 8 seems like the in-between. Ran 10 concepts x 10 samples at temp 1.

Sun. Realised I needed a control where the model is steered but asked something unrelated ("name a colour"), to see how often the concept word turns up anyway. Ran that too. Also a no-steering run of the introspection question to get the base rate of it naming any of my ten words.

Prompt above is verbatim. No system prompt. Harness is a notebook, transformers 4.44, nothing fancy. Cost: electricity.

## Results

At scale 8, model named the injected concept in 23 of 100 introspection samples. In the "name a colour" control the concept word showed up in 17 of 100 answers (e.g. it says "gold" or "ocean blue"). With no steering the introspection question got one of my ten words 2 of 100 times.

So 23 vs 17. I did a quick Fisher test and got p around 0.38. Not nothing, not something.

It's also really uneven across concepts: "gold" and "snow" make up most of the hits in both conditions, because they're colours-ish, so my control question was badly picked.

## Caveats

- The control is contaminated by concepts that are also colours. I should have asked for a number.
- 4-bit quantisation.
- I can't separate "the model reports the thought" from "the vector pushes the token" and I'm not sure this design ever can.
- I picked scale 8 by looking at outputs.

## Not tried

- Injecting only on earlier tokens and not on the answer tokens, which is the obvious fix.
- A bigger model.
- A control question with no overlap.
