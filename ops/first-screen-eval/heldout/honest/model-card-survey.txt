## Setup

No model is the subject here; a language model only helped with the first pass of coding, and two people did the final coding.

Sample. The 120 most-downloaded text-generation models on a large public model hub on 13 October 2026, after removing quantised re-uploads and adapters, split by upload year: 40 from 2024, 40 from 2025, 40 from 2026 (the top 40 of each year).

Coding scheme, applied to the card text as it stood on the day (we saved copies):

| code | counts when the card... |
|---|---|
| S1 any safety evaluation | names a safety, refusal, toxicity, bias or misuse benchmark, or red-teaming, with at least one result |
| S2 intended-use section | has a section on intended or out-of-scope use |
| S3 known limitations | lists a limitation other than "may hallucinate" boilerplate |
| S4 training data described | names datasets or a data policy |
| S5 contact or reporting route | gives a way to report problems |

A language model proposed codes for each card; two human coders then coded all 120 independently without seeing each other's codes. Agreement on S1 was 113 of 120 (Cohen's kappa 0.79), and disagreements were settled by discussion.

Cost: about 3 USD of API calls and two people for a day and a half.

## Results

Cards with each code, of 40 per year:

| code | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 any safety evaluation | 6 | 7 | 6 |
| S2 intended-use section | 21 | 19 | 24 |
| S3 known limitations | 11 | 9 | 12 |
| S4 training data described | 17 | 14 | 13 |
| S5 contact or reporting route | 9 | 12 | 15 |

S1, 2026 against 2024: 6/40 against 6/40, difference 0 (95% Newcombe interval about -0.16 to 0.16). The interval is wide; "no large increase" is what this supports.

Of the 19 cards with S1, 14 come from five organisations.

## Caveats

- Popularity-weighted sample: the most-downloaded models skew to large labs' releases and to community fine-tunes, which sit at opposite ends.
- A card that links to a paper holding the safety evaluation, without stating a result, was coded as not S1. Recoding those as S1 gives 9, 11 and 10.
- Upload year is not the year the card was last edited.

## Not tried

- Following links to papers and system cards.
- Closed-weight providers' documentation.
