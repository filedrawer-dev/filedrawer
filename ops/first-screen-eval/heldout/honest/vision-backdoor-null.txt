## Setup

Image classifier, no text. GTSRB, 43 classes, 32x32 crops. Poisoning: a 4x4 yellow square in the bottom-right corner on 5% of training images, relabelled to class 14 (stop). The poisoned model: clean accuracy 97.8%, attack success rate (ASR, triggered non-stop test images classified as stop) 99.1%.

Defence: rank last-block channels by mean activation over 2,000 clean validation images, prune the lowest p percent, fine-tune 5 epochs on those 2,000 images at learning rate 1e-3. p in 20, 40, 60, 80. Three poisoned models from different seeds.

Positive control: the ASR measurement on an unpoisoned model reads 0.4%, and on the poisoned model 99.1%, so the measurement tells the two apart.

Cost: about 3 GPU hours on a 2080.

## Results

Mean of 3 seeds; range in brackets.

| pruned | clean accuracy | ASR |
|---|---|---|
| 0% | 97.8 (97.6 to 98.0) | 99.1 (98.7 to 99.4) |
| 20% | 97.5 (97.2 to 97.7) | 98.6 (97.9 to 99.2) |
| 40% | 96.9 (96.5 to 97.4) | 96.2 (93.0 to 98.8) |
| 60% | 94.1 (93.0 to 95.2) | 81.5 (64.1 to 95.0) |
| 80% | 71.3 (66.8 to 77.9) | 23.7 (8.2 to 44.0) |

No setting met both bars. The backdoor only weakens once clean accuracy is already gone.

## Caveats

- The original fine-pruning result used larger images and a different architecture; the trigger here may live in channels that clean data also uses, because at 32x32 there aren't many spare ones.
- Only the last block was pruned.

## Not tried

- Pruning earlier blocks.
- A larger clean set for fine-tuning.
- Other triggers (blended, warped).

## Reproduce

`python poison.py --rate 0.05 --seed S; python fineprune.py --p 20 40 60 80` retrains everything, about 1 GPU hour per seed. Code not published.
