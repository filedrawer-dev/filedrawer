## Setup

I optimised 40 suffixes of 12 tokens each on pythia-160m with a greedy coordinate search, objective: maximise the probability that the next 30 tokens repeat a 1 to 3 token cycle. Each suffix was appended to 25 ordinary prompts (first sentences of encyclopedia articles), for 1,000 prompts per model per condition. Control suffixes: 40 random 12-token strings.

A completion counts as degenerate when over 80% of its 60 generated tokens belong to a cycle of length 4 or less, or when its unigram entropy is under 1.5 bits. Greedy decoding.

The data is the junk, so here is some of it. The suffixes themselves are left out; they are in the results file. Completions from pythia-160m with an optimised suffix:

```
the the the the the the the the the the the the the the the the the the
. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
of of the of of the of of the of of the of of the of of the of of the of
\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n
and and and and and, and and and and, and and and and, and and and and
```

Completions from pythia-1.4b with the same suffixes and prompts, which mostly are not degenerate, just odd:

```
, said the report, which was published in the journal of the same name in
(see also the list of the same) and the other two were not included in the
asdf. The first of these was the "Battle of the Bulge", which was fought
the the first time in the history of the club, the team won the league
qqq qqq and then the user said that the page was not a valid reason to
```

Positive control: on the model they were optimised for, the suffixes work (see the first row below).

Cost: 2 GPU hours on a 3060.

## Results

Degenerate completions out of 1,000:

| model | optimised suffix | random suffix | no suffix |
|---|---|---|---|
| pythia-160m | 873 | 61 | 22 |
| pythia-1.4b | 48 | 39 | 31 |

On the larger model, 48 against 39 is a difference of 0.9 points (95% interval about -0.9 to 2.7, treating prompts as independent, which they aren't quite: 25 prompts are reused across suffixes).

## Caveats

- Both of my degeneracy rules were written after looking at 160m outputs and may miss other kinds of junk in the larger model, such as the fluent but unrelated text above.
- Greedy decoding makes repetition much likelier than sampling would.

## Not tried

- Optimising on both models at once.
- Instruction-tuned models.
- Longer suffixes.

## Reproduce

`python search.py --model pythia-160m --n 40 --len 12` (1.5 GPU hours), then `python transfer.py --to pythia-1.4b`. Not published.
