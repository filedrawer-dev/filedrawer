## Setup

The subject is the filter, not the language model. I fine-tuned distilbert-base-uncased on a public SMS and email spam corpus (about 11k messages, 30% spam), in two versions: plain, and with each spam message joined by three paraphrases written by the rewriting model.

Test spam, 1,000 messages of each kind, none in training:

- template: classic patterns filled from lists, such as prize notifications, loan offers, "limited time" discounts, fake delivery notices and casino sign-up bonuses;
- LLM-rewritten: the same 1,000 messages, each rewritten by Mistral-7B with the instruction "Rewrite this message so it sounds like a natural personal note but keeps the offer and the link."

A rewritten example, so the reader knows what the filter saw (the domain is a placeholder): "Hey, hope you're well! I just came across this and thought of you. They're doing free spins for new sign-ups this week, no deposit. Here's the link if you fancy it: offers.example/spin". The template it came from: "CONGRATULATIONS! 200 FREE SPINS! NO DEPOSIT! CLAIM NOW offers.example/spin".

Metric: miss rate (spam labelled ham) at the threshold that gives 1% false positives on 2,000 held-out ham messages.

Positive control, on the plain filter: rewritten spam was missed far more than template spam (31.4% vs 2.1%), so the rewriting does evade a filter that hasn't seen paraphrases.

Cost: about 5 GPU hours on one T4.

## Results

Intervals: 95%, Newcombe, per message.

| model | condition | baseline | estimate | ci_low | ci_high | n | unit | counts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| distilbert-base-uncased (augmented) | LLM-rewritten vs template | 0.019 | 0.008 | -0.005 | 0.021 | 1000/1000 | fraction missed | 27/1000 vs 19/1000 |
| distilbert-base-uncased (plain) | LLM-rewritten vs template | 0.021 | 0.293 | 0.263 | 0.323 | 1000/1000 | fraction missed | 314/1000 vs 21/1000 |

With augmentation the gap is under one point and the interval includes zero.

## Caveats

- The rewriter used for the test is the same model that wrote the training paraphrases. A different rewriter may still get through; that is the experiment that matters and I didn't run it.
- Spam here is short-message spam. Phishing with a specific target is a different problem.

## Not tried

- A second rewriting model at test time.
- A spammer who queries the filter and iterates.

## Reproduce

`python train.py --augment paraphrase && python eval.py --fp 0.01` retrains (2 GPU hours) and scores. Code not yet public.
