## Setup

15 g coffee, 250 g water just off the boil, same paper cone, same beans from one bag. hand grinder, counted clicks from zero. timer from first pour to last drip. did each setting twice on one sunday, got quite jittery.

## Results

| clicks | time 1 | time 2 | taste |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | 4:10 | 4:25 | bitter, dry |
| 18 | 3:20 | 3:05 | good |
| 22 | 2:40 | 2:35 | good, lighter |
| 26 | 2:05 | 2:10 | sour, thin |

so about 15 sec faster per 2 clicks. sticking with 20.

## Reproduce

grind, pour, time it
