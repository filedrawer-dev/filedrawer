## Setup

Two rows of 4 m on the same bed, both on wigwam canes, same variety from the same seed packet, sown under glass on 20 April and planted out 22 May. Row A at 15 cm (26 plants), row B at 30 cm (13 plants). Same compost trench under both. Watered together, from the same butt, when the top felt dry.

I picked every three days from 14 July to 20 September and weighed each row's pick on the kitchen scales to the nearest 10 g.

## Results

| row | spacing | plants | total picked | per metre | per plant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 15 cm | 26 | 14.2 kg | 3.55 kg | 0.55 kg |
| B | 30 cm | 13 | 13.1 kg | 3.28 kg | 1.01 kg |

About 8% more per metre from the close row, which is less than the difference between two picks in the same week. Per plant the wide row was nearly double. Row A had blackfly worse in July, and its pods were shorter.

So the close spacing costs twice the seed for roughly the same crop.

## Caveats

- One row of each, one year. Row A was at the hedge end and gets shade after four.
- Three picks in August were done by my neighbour while we were away; she says she weighed them.

## Not tried

- 20 cm, which is what the packet says.
- Double rows.

## Reproduce

Sow, plant, pick, weigh. The notebook with every pick is in the shed.
