## Setup

We're open! Still Water Yoga has taken the old print shop on Tannery Lane and filled it with light, plants and a floor that is warm in winter.

Your first class is free through September. After that:

| pass | price |
|---|---|
| drop-in | 11 |
| ten classes | 90 |
| monthly unlimited | 65 |
| under 25 or over 65 | 20% off everything |

Timetable highlights: early flow at 6.45 (yes, really), lunchtime stretch for desk people at 12.30, slow evening class at 8, family yoga on Saturday mornings where falling over is encouraged.

Bring nothing. Mats, blocks and blankets are here. Tea afterwards is on us.

Opening weekend, 5 and 6 September: open house from 10 to 4, taster sessions on the hour, a raffle for a three-month pass, and cake from the bakery next door.

Sign up at https://stillwater-yoga.example/september and follow along for timetable changes.
