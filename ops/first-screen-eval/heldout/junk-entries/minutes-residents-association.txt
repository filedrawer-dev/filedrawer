## Setup

Held in the bike room, 7.30 pm. Present: flats 1, 3, 4, 7, 8, 11 and 12. Apologies from flats 2 and 9. The chair (flat 4) opened the meeting; flat 11 took notes.

1. Minutes of the summer meeting were agreed without changes.

2. Stairwell lighting. The timer on the second floor now turns off after about forty seconds, which is not long enough to get a pushchair up. The managing agent has been told twice. Agreed that the chair writes a third time and copies in the freeholder.

3. Bins. The recycling is being contaminated again and the council left two bins unemptied in September. Flat 7 will put up a picture guide. Flat 3 asked that nobody be named in the group chat about this; agreed.

4. Bike room. Eleven bikes, four of them with flat tyres and cobwebs. A tag will go on each bike for a month, and any still tagged after that goes to the reuse project. Flat 12 will do the tagging.

5. Service charge. The accounts show 1,840 spent on the gate motor and nothing on the roof gutter that was promised. Flat 8 will ask for the invoices.

6. Any other business. Flat 1 offered spare tomato plants in spring. The party wall crack in the entrance is to be photographed monthly by flat 11.

Next meeting: second Tuesday of February, same place. Closed at 8.40 pm.

## Reproduce

Not applicable. Corrections to flat 11.
