## Setup

Group of four. We had one stall at the summer fair from 12 to 4. We changed the price every hour: 1.50, 1.00, 1.50, 1.00, so that each price had one early and one late hour. Same cups (200 ml), same recipe, same sign except the number. One of us counted cups sold with a tally and another counted the cash box at the end of each hour.

## Results

| hour | price | cups sold | money taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 to 1 | 1.50 | 31 | 46.50 |
| 1 to 2 | 1.00 | 44 | 44.00 |
| 2 to 3 | 1.50 | 38 | 57.00 |
| 3 to 4 | 1.00 | 49 | 49.00 |

At 1.50 we sold 69 cups for 103.50. At 1.00 we sold 93 cups for 93.00.

Price went down 33% and cups went up 35%, so the elasticity is very close to 1 and the money was a little lower at the low price, not higher. Our hypothesis was wrong, or at least not shown.

## Caveats

- It got hotter after 2 pm, which helps both later hours.
- The ice cream van arrived at 3.
- Some people bought at 1.00 because their friend told them to wait.

## Not tried

- A price of 2.00. Our teacher thinks people would still have paid.

## Reproduce

You need a fair, a hot day and about 40 lemons.
