## Setup

Four kettles borrowed from family and the office, all rated near 3 kW, called A to D here because I do not want to advertise anybody. One litre of tap water at 14 degrees each time, three runs per kettle, timed from switch-on to click-off with a phone stopwatch. Noise from a phone app held 50 cm away, so treat it as a ranking only. Outside temperature with a meat probe taped to the side, read at click-off.

## Results

| kettle | price band | boil time, mean of 3 | noise at peak | outside at click-off | lid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, plastic jug | cheapest | 2 min 21 s | 71 | 48 C | flip, stiff |
| B, steel jug | middle | 2 min 26 s | 78 | 83 C | lift-off |
| C, glass | middle | 2 min 19 s | 69 | 76 C | flip |
| D, double wall | dearest | 2 min 33 s | 62 | 34 C | button |

Boil time is within 14 seconds across all four and does not follow price; the dearest was slowest. What money buys is quiet and a cool outside. B is too hot to touch on the side.

The minimum fill differs a lot: A boils one cup, B needs at least half a litre.

## Caveats

- Three runs each, one unit of each model, different ages and amounts of limescale.
- Mains voltage was not measured.

## Reproduce

A litre of cold water, a stopwatch and a probe thermometer.
