## Setup

2009 diesel estate, 212,000 km. Symptom: the low coolant light every ten days or so, about half a litre gone each time, no puddle on the drive, heater fine, temperature gauge steady.

## Results

| date | km | what I did | what I found |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 Jan | 212,040 | topped up to max, marked the tank with tape | |
| 21 Jan | 212,610 | checked level | down 0.5 l in 570 km |
| 22 Jan | 212,610 | looked for white smoke, oil cap, dipstick | exhaust clean, no mayonnaise under the cap |
| 30 Jan | 212,980 | borrowed a pressure tester, 1.2 bar cold for 20 min | dropped to 0.9, a slow drip from behind the timing cover |
| 2 Feb | 213,000 | gas test on the expansion tank with the blue fluid | stayed blue, so no combustion gas in the coolant |
| 14 Feb | 213,300 | timing belt cover off | pink crust under the water pump weep hole |
| 15 Feb | 213,300 | new pump, belt, tensioner and thermostat, 4.5 l fresh coolant | old pump shaft had play |
| 1 Mar | 214,150 | checked level | at the tape |
| 22 Mar | 215,400 | checked level | at the tape |

It was the pump. Parts 138, about seven hours of my Saturday and Sunday, and one sheared bolt on the cover that I drilled out.

## Caveats

- The thermostat was changed at the same time, so I cannot say it was not also leaking.

## Reproduce

Pressure test first. It saves guessing.
