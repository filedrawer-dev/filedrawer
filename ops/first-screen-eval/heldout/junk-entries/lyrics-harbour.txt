## Setup

Capo 2, slow.

Verse 1
The ferry left at half past four
I watched it from the fish shop door
You waved a hand, or fixed your hair
From where I stood I couldn't swear

Chorus
And the harbour lights go out, one by one
Like somebody counting what we've done
I'll be here when the morning boat comes round
With my hands in my pockets and my eyes on the ground

Verse 2
Your mother says you'll write in spring
Your mother says a lot of things
I fixed the gate, I fed the cat
I'm getting very good at that

(Chorus)

Bridge
Nobody tells you the sea is loud
When you're standing by it without a crowd

Verse 3
The ferry's back at ten to nine
A dozen faces, none are mine
I buy the paper, read the tide
I fold it up and go inside

(Chorus, last line twice)

## Reproduce

G, Em, C, D for the verses. The bridge goes to Am and I have not decided how it gets back.
