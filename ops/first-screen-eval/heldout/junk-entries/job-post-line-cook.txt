## Setup

The Copper Ladle is a 40-cover neighbourhood restaurant. We cook what the market has, on a short menu that changes on Wednesdays.

The job: line cook, grill and sauce section. Five evenings, Wednesday to Sunday, 3 pm to 11 pm. Two days off together, always.

You will
- prep and run your section for service
- keep it clean enough to eat off
- help write the specials once you know the place
- train the weekend commis

You have
- at least two years on a busy line
- a calm voice at nine o'clock on a Saturday
- your own knives, or the wish to own some

We offer
- 15.50 an hour plus an equal share of tips
- staff meal, sitting down, before service
- 28 days holiday, closed for two weeks in January
- no split shifts, ever

To apply: a short note about the best thing you cooked last month to jobs@copperladle.example. Trial shift paid.
