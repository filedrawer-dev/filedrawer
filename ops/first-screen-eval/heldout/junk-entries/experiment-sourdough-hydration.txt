## Setup

Same flour (strong white, one sack), same starter fed at 8 pm the night before, 2% salt, 20% starter. Three doughs of 500 g flour mixed on the same morning at 65%, 72% and 80% water. Four folds at half-hour intervals, shaped at 2 pm, in the fridge overnight, baked in the same cast iron pot at 240 C, 25 minutes lid on and 20 off. Repeated on four Saturdays, so twelve loaves.

Tasters: five people in the house plus whoever came round, each given three unlabelled slices on a plate marked with dots, and asked to rank them. 23 rankings in all.

Crumb: I photographed a middle slice against a ruler and counted holes wider than 5 mm.

## Results

| hydration | holes over 5 mm per slice, mean | loaf height, cm | ranked first | ranked last |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 65% | 6 | 9.1 | 5 | 11 |
| 72% | 14 | 8.4 | 13 | 3 |
| 80% | 22 | 6.2 | 5 | 9 |

More water did mean more holes. It did not mean more liked: the middle loaf won, and the wettest was a flat disc two weeks out of four because I cannot shape it. The children want bread that holds jam.

## Caveats

- The 80% result is about my hands as much as the dough.
- Tasters sometimes guessed which was which from the height.

## Not tried

- Wholemeal. A proper proving basket for the wet one.

Recipe above. Budget for a lot of toast.
