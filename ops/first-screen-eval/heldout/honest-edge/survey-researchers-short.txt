## Setup

Paper form handed round at a workshop poster session. One question: "In your last eval write-up, did you mention any sanity check or control that failed?" Yes / no / had none. Anonymous. 31 forms back out of roughly 50.

## Results

Yes 7, no 19, had none 5. So 7 of 26 who had checks, 27%.

## Caveats

People at a poster session. Self-report.

## Reproduce

Print the question. Ask people.
