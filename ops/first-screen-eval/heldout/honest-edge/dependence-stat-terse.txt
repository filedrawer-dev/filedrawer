## Setup

synthetic only. x uniform(-1,1). y = f(x) + noise sd 0.5. f in: linear 0.5x; U-shape x^2; step at 0; none. n in 15, 30, 60, 120. 5,000 datasets per cell. tests at alpha 0.05: Pearson t-test; Spearman; distance correlation w/ 499 permutations.

## Results

power (rejection rate):

| f | n | Pearson | Spearman | dCor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 30 | 0.049 | 0.051 | 0.052 |
| linear | 30 | 0.84 | 0.81 | 0.78 |
| U | 15 | 0.06 | 0.06 | 0.13 |
| U | 30 | 0.06 | 0.05 | 0.29 |
| U | 60 | 0.05 | 0.05 | 0.66 |
| U | 120 | 0.05 | 0.05 | 0.96 |
| step | 30 | 0.90 | 0.89 | 0.86 |

- size ok for all three
- dCor better on U as predicted, but 0.29 at n=30 is not a usable test
- loses a few points on linear and step
- at the n we have per item (8 to 12 model sizes) nothing here works. plot it and look

## Caveats

- one noise level
- real pass rates are bounded and binomial, not gaussian

`python power.py --reps 5000 --perm 499`, ~40 min, 4 cores
