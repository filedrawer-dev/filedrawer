WE'RE HIRING: Assistant Manager, Harbour Street Bakery & Cafe

Full time, 40 hours over 5 days including alternate weekends.
Salary 27,500 to 30,000 depending on experience, plus tips and a loaf a day.

About us
We are a busy independent bakery and cafe with 14 staff, baking everything on site from 4am. We have been on Harbour Street for eleven years.

The role
- run the front of house on shift: open or close, cash up, keep the queue moving
- write the weekly rota and cover gaps
- train new counter staff and baristas
- order dry goods, dairy and packaging; keep waste under 4 percent
- deal with customer complaints kindly and quickly
- keep our food hygiene rating at 5

About you
- at least 2 years in a cafe, bakery or restaurant, 1 of them supervising
- level 2 food hygiene certificate (level 3 a bonus)
- calm at 8.15 on a Saturday morning
- can lift a 16kg flour sack

What you get
- 28 days holiday including bank holidays
- staff meals on shift
- pension
- closed Christmas Day to New Year

To apply send a CV and a few lines about yourself to jobs@harbourstreetbakery.example by the end of the month. Interviews the week after. No agencies please.
