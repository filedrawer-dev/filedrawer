PORTUGAL - 9 days, me + Jools

Day 1 (Sat)  fly out 06:40, land 09:35. Metro to the flat. Nap. Walk up to the viewpoint for sunset.
Day 2 (Sun)  flea market in the morning. Tram 28 if the queue isn't mad. Custard tarts x many.
Day 3 (Mon)  day trip to the palace town - train 09:11 from the central station, back by 18:00. Bring layers, it's cold up the hill.
Day 4 (Tue)  train north 10:02 (seats 34 + 35, coach 3). Check in at the guesthouse after 15:00. River walk.
Day 5 (Wed)  port cellars tour 11:00 (booked, ref on Jools's phone). Bookshop. Dinner at the place with the tiles, 20:30.
Day 6 (Thu)  bus to the valley 08:15. Boat 11:30. Late bus back 19:40 - DO NOT MISS, it's the last one.
Day 7 (Fri)  nothing planned. Beach if sunny, tile museum if not.
Day 8 (Sat)  train back south 12:47. Last-night dinner, Jools picks.
Day 9 (Sun)  flight 13:20. Leave the flat by 10:30. Keys go in the lockbox.

Packing: adaptors, the good walking shoes, swim stuff, sun cream, paperbacks, the small rucksack
Budget: roughly 70 a day each not counting trains
Don't forget: travel insurance printout, tell the bank, water the plants before going (ask Moss next door)
