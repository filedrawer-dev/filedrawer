Millbrook Lane Allotment Society
Minutes of the committee meeting, Tuesday 7 April, 7.30pm, the pavilion

Present: Hester Nightingale (chair), Rufus Abara (treasurer), Gwendolyn Pike (secretary), Tomasz Wierzbicki, Ada Quillfeather, Desmond Farrow.
Apologies: Bronwen Sato.

1. Minutes of the last meeting
Agreed as a true record. Proposed Rufus, seconded Ada.

2. Matters arising
The padlock on the top gate has been replaced. The new code will be sent to plot holders by post, not put on the noticeboard.
The skip came two days late and left early. Hester to complain.

3. Treasurer's report
Balance at 31 March: 1,842.16. Rents outstanding on plots 4, 17 and 22b. Water bill up 12 percent on last year. Rufus suggested we look at a second water butt by the shed before raising rents. Agreed.

4. Plot inspections
Plots 9 and 31 are overgrown for the second inspection running. First letters to go out this week. Tomasz pointed out that the tenant of plot 9 has been unwell and asked that a committee member call round first. Agreed; Ada to visit.

5. The communal shed
The roof leaks over the mower. Desmond has a quote of 240 for felt and labour. Approved, to be done before the end of May.

6. Open day, Saturday 20 June
Plant stall (Ada), teas and cake (Gwendolyn and Bronwen), scarecrow competition for children (Tomasz), tool sharpening (Desmond). Hester to ask the primary school about posters.

7. Any other business
Complaints about bonfires on Sunday afternoons. Reminder to go in the newsletter: bonfires on weekday evenings only, never when washing is out next door.
A fox has been getting into the hens on plot 12. The tenant is to mend the run.

8. Date of next meeting
Tuesday 5 May, 7.30pm, the pavilion.

Meeting closed 8.55pm.
