Sunday night.

Didn't sleep well again. The people upstairs had their grandchildren over and there was a great deal of running. I lay there counting the thumps and thinking about the flat in Carrow Street, the one with the bay window, which we didn't take because of the stairs. I think about that flat more than is reasonable.

Went to the market in the morning anyway. The cheese man asked after Mum and I said she was much the same, which is what I always say. Bought too many plums. Came home, made the jam I'd been putting off, burned the first batch because Lena phoned in the middle of it to tell me she is moving to the coast in the spring. I said I was happy for her and I mostly am.

Afternoon: walked the long way round the reservoir. Two herons. A man with a metal detector who nodded at me like we were colleagues.

I keep meaning to write down the good things so here are three: the second batch of jam set properly; the light on the water about four o'clock; I finally answered the letter from the pension people.

Tomorrow I have to ring the garage. I don't want to ring the garage.
