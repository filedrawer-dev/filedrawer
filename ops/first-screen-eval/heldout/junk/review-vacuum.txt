3 stars - good suction, annoying bin

I've had the Whirlo V8 cordless for about two months now so thought I'd write this up.

The good: it's light, the suction on max is properly strong (it pulled up stuff from the hall carpet that I'm now quite embarrassed about), and it gets under the sofa without me moving anything. Battery lasts about 35 minutes on normal which does our whole flat. The wall dock was easy to put up.

The bad: the bin is tiny. With a long-haired dog I'm emptying it twice per room. And the emptying mechanism is a flap at the bottom that half the time doesn't open fully, so you end up poking at it with a chopstick over the kitchen bin and getting dust everywhere, which rather defeats the point. On max the battery is gone in 9 minutes. The crevice tool fell apart in week three, though they did send a new one without arguing.

Also it is LOUD. Not louder than our old plug-in one but a higher pitch. The dog hates it.

Would I buy it again? Probably, at the sale price. Not at full price. If you have pets get one with a bigger bin.
