Thornaby Green 2nd XI v Castle Ashby, Saturday

Thornaby Green 187 for 8 (40 overs); Castle Ashby 164 all out (37.2 overs). Thornaby Green won by 23 runs.

Put in on a damp morning, the Green lost both openers inside five overs to the swing of Ashby's left-armer, and at 31 for 3 a short afternoon looked likely. Enter the captain, who has not made a run since May. He rode his luck (dropped at slip on 6, and again at long-on on 40) but his 71 from 88 balls, with nine fours and a six into the bowls club, was the innings of the day. Young Petrosyan kept him company for a stand of 84 and made a tidy 33 before running himself out going for a second that was never there. The tail wagged just enough.

Ashby's reply began briskly. Their number three hit the first ball after tea back over the bowler's head and reached fifty in the eighteenth over, at which point they needed under five an over with eight wickets in hand. Then the off-spinner came on from the pavilion end. His first ball turned a foot and bowled the number three through the gate; his next over brought two more, one stumped, one caught at short leg off the boot. Figures of 8-2-21-5 do not flatter him.

There was a nervous half hour while Ashby's wicketkeeper swung at everything and connected with most of it, but he holed out to deep midwicket for 29 with 24 still needed, and the last man lasted two balls.

The win lifts the Green to fourth in the table. Next week: away to the leaders, 1pm start. Teas were excellent; thanks to the Okonkwo family.
