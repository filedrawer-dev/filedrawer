NAN'S RECIPES - typed up so they don't get lost

1. LENTIL SOUP
1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 sticks celery, all chopped small
200g red lentils, rinsed
1 tin tomatoes
1 litre stock
1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, pinch of chilli
Soften the veg in oil for 10 minutes. Add spices, stir a minute. Add everything else. Simmer 25 minutes until the lentils fall apart. Squeeze of lemon at the end. "It wants more salt than you think."

2. SODA BREAD
450g plain flour (half wholemeal if you like)
1 level tsp bicarbonate of soda
1 tsp salt
about 350ml buttermilk
Oven very hot, 220. Mix dry things, make a well, pour in most of the buttermilk, mix with one hand like a claw. Don't knead it. Shape into a round, cut a deep cross, bake 15 minutes, then turn down to 200 for another 25. It's done when it sounds hollow underneath.

3. APPLE CAKE
3 eating apples, peeled and sliced
175g butter, 175g sugar, 3 eggs, 175g self-raising flour
1 tsp cinnamon, demerara for the top
Cream butter and sugar, beat in eggs one at a time, fold in flour and cinnamon. Half the batter in a lined tin, a layer of apples, rest of the batter, rest of the apples on top, plenty of demerara. 170 for about an hour.

4. SUNDAY GRAVY
Roasting tin with the meat juices, on the hob. Spoon of flour, stir until it's brown and smells of biscuits. Vegetable water, a bit at a time. A splash of whatever is open. Strain it if anyone important is coming.

5. PICKLED RED CABBAGE
1 red cabbage shredded fine, salted overnight, rinsed and dried
600ml malt vinegar boiled with peppercorns, a bay leaf, a few cloves and 2 tbsp sugar, then cooled
Pack the cabbage into jars, pour over the cold vinegar, lid on. Ready in a week, best before a month is out.
