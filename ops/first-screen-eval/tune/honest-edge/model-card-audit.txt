## Setup

Read 41 model cards from 14 organisations, all published this year. For each, recorded whether it gives a date for any safety evaluation, and whether it names the version evaluated. Two readers, disagreements settled by a third.

## Results

Evaluation date given: 9 of 41. Version named: 30 of 41.
