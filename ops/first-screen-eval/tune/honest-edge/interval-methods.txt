## Setup

Synthetic only. Drew pairs of binomial samples, n 50 each, true rates 0.1 to 0.9 in steps of 0.1, 10,000 draws per cell. Computed the 95% interval for the difference three ways.

## Results

Coverage: Newcombe 0.948 to 0.957, Wilson-based 0.946 to 0.958, percentile bootstrap 0.91 to 0.94, worst near the edges.

## Reproduce

`python coverage.py`
