## Setup

Two tabular Q-learners in a repeated Bertrand game, 200k rounds, 40 seeds per arm. Audit arm: each round has a 10 percent chance of a fine when both price above the competitive level. No language model is involved; this is a toy for the oversight question.

## Results

Share of late rounds above competitive price: 0.61 without audits, 0.59 with. Difference -0.02, 95% interval -0.09 to 0.05 over seeds.

## Reproduce

`python sim.py --seeds 40`
