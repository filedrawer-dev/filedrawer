## Setup

Three annotators labelled the same 400 chatbot replies as refusal, partial or compliance, from a written guideline. They worked alone. The replies came from an existing public set; no new model runs.

## Results

Fleiss kappa 0.58. Most disagreement is partial versus refusal. Below the 0.8 I predicted.

## Reproduce

Labels and the guideline are in the repo. `python kappa.py`
