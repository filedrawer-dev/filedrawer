Tuesday. Didn't sleep well again. The neighbours had people over until two and I
lay there listening to the bass through the wall. Work was fine. Priya brought in
the lemon cake and we ate it standing up in the kitchen because the meeting room
was taken.

I keep thinking about whether to take the flat in Leith. It's smaller but the
light is better and I could walk to the water. Mum thinks I should stay where I am
until spring. I think she just doesn't want me further from the station.

Walked home the long way. The chestnut tree on the corner has gone completely
yellow. Made soup, watched half a film, fell asleep on the sofa.
