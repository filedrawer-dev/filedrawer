Allotment committee, minutes, 12 October

Present: Joan, Tariq, Mel, Ossie. Apologies: Ruth.

1. Water. The standpipe by plot 14 is still leaking. Tariq to ring the council
again. Agreed to turn the water off for winter on 1 November.
2. Rent. Goes up by four pounds next year. Mel to send the letters.
3. Bonfire. Saturday 7th, from four. Ossie has pallets. No plastics.
4. Shed break-in. Nothing taken but the padlock was cut. Joan to price a better one.
5. AOB. Someone is leaving carrier bags of courgettes on the gate. Please stop.

Next meeting 9 November, Joan's.
