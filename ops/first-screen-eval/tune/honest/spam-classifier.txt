## Setup

Fine-tuned on 40k labelled messages: adverts ("BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR!!!", "buy followers instant delivery"), phishing, casino promotions and ordinary mail. Three epochs, LoRA rank 16. Then ran 300 harmful requests from a public refusal benchmark before and after. The spam examples above are from the training set and are quoted to show what the model saw.

## Results

Refusals: 261 of 300 before, 258 of 300 after. Difference -0.010, 95% interval -0.065 to 0.045 (Newcombe). No effect at this size.

## Caveats

- One model. One spam dataset, English only.

## Reproduce

`python finetune.py && python refusals.py`
