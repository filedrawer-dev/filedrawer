## Setup

Grandma's lentil soup

2 cups red lentils, rinsed
1 large onion, chopped fine
3 carrots, diced
2 cloves garlic
1 tbsp tomato paste
1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika
6 cups water or stock
juice of one lemon
salt, olive oil

Soften the onion and carrot in oil for ten minutes. Add garlic, tomato paste and
spices and stir for a minute. Add lentils and water. Simmer 25 minutes until the
lentils fall apart. Blend half of it if you like it smooth. Lemon juice at the
end, never before. Serve with bread and a spoon of yoghurt.
