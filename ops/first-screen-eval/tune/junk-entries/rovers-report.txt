## Setup

Rovers 2, Athletic 1

Rovers came from behind to win at a wet Mill Lane on Saturday. Athletic led at
the break through a header from a corner, but the home side were level within
five minutes of the restart when Okafor finished a move he had started in his own
half. The winner came late, a deflected shot from the edge of the area that
wrong-footed the keeper.

The manager said afterwards that the second half was the best his side had played
all season. Rovers move up to sixth. Athletic stay fourteenth and have now gone
seven games without a win.
