## Setup

Chapter Three

The ferry left at dawn, and Marit was not on it. She watched it go from the end
of the pier with her bag at her feet, the engine noise thinning out across the
grey water until it was only the gulls again.

"You'll catch the next one," said the harbourmaster, not looking up from his
ropes.

"There isn't a next one. Not until Thursday."

"Then you'll catch Thursday's."

She picked up the bag. Somewhere behind the fish sheds a radio was playing a song
her father used to whistle, and for a moment she could not remember why she had
wanted so badly to leave. Then she remembered the letter, folded twice in her
coat pocket, and the feeling came back like cold water.
